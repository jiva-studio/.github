Dobar dan! We are a team of passionate developers creating software that aids
in learning, deep thinking, and spiritual development. Our mission is to
empower people to become their best selves and achieve their full potential.

We believe personal growth is a continuous journey, and technology can play a
meaningful role in this process. From note-taking and knowledge synthesis to
synchronized audio study and structured education, we build thoughtful,
private, and focused tools.

## Projects

- 🌿 **[Numen](https://github.com/jiva-studio/numen)** — A private, local-first
  workspace for notes, connected memory, and spaced repetition.
- 🎧 **[Shruti](https://github.com/jiva-studio/shruti)** — Audio lecture player
  with synchronized transcripts, bookmarks, and AI study companion.
- 📚 **[Vidya](https://github.com/jiva-studio/vidya)** — Learning Management
  System (LMS) for structured courses and study.
- 🤖 **[Band](https://github.com/jiva-studio/band)** — Deterministic
  multi-agent orchestration framework.
